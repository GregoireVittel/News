Correlations appear once 20 trading sessions have both news and a CAC 40 close (now 10). Fewer sessions would mostly show chance.

How this is measured

  • Stories: France and Global Markets sections only, in all languages, from publishers' headlines and RSS snippets.
  • Tone: positive minus negative market words (e.g. hausse, rebond, rally / baisse, chute, plunge, spadek) divided by all such words, from −100 to +100. Zones: Stormy below −40, Rain −40 to −10, Changeable −10 to +10, Fair +10 to +40, Sunny above +40. Word lists miss irony and negation, so read it as a rough gauge.
  • Sessions: news published after the 17:30 Paris close counts toward the next trading session; weekend news counts toward Monday. Sessions with fewer than 5 stories are left out.
  • CAC 40: daily closes from Yahoo Finance, with Stooq as a fallback; the latest value may be intraday while the market is open.
  • Read with care: a correlation shows the two moving together, not that news moves the market. News also reacts to the market, and checking many themes turns up some patterns by chance.
